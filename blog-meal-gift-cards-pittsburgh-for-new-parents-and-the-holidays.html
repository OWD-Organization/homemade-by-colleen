Some gifts end up in a drawer. A homemade meal gets eaten, savored, and remembered. A Homemade by Colleen gift card gives someone in Pittsburgh a fridge full of comfort, and a night off from cooking.

When I cannot take someone to dinner, or go to their house and cook a week's worth of meals myself, a gift card is the next best thing. They choose the meals. We cook them fresh. We deliver them in the South Hills.

Why a meal gift card is a gift people actually use

A gift card for homemade food is practical, and it still feels personal. The recipient picks what they want from the weekly menu. There is no subscription to manage, and no pressure to use it all at once.

That makes it a strong gift when you are not sure what someone needs. It works for a new baby, a busy season, a hard week, or a simple thank-you.

Our meals are fresh and never frozen, cooked with local ingredients whenever we can. You can read more about that in Fresh Local Ingredients for Meal Prep in Pittsburgh. The food is meant to taste like it came from a family kitchen, not a factory.

A thoughtful gift for new parents

The most-requested gift for a new baby is not another onesie. It is a meal they did not have to cook.

Those first weeks are full of feedings, visitors, and very little sleep. Grocery trips and dinner plans slip down the list. A meal gift card lets new parents order when they need help, not on someone else's schedule.

They can add meals, skip a week, or use the remaining balance later. The code keeps what is left, so the gift can cover more than one order. That is useful when energy comes and goes.

If you live nearby and want to help in a more direct way, you can also contact us about catering for a shower. For the weeks after the baby arrives, a gift card is often the kinder follow-up.

A holiday gift for busy families, teachers, and friends

The holidays put a lot of names on a list. A meal gift card fits many of them.

Busy families. Give a weeknight back to the family that is always moving between work, practice, and homework. They can order Sunday or Wednesday delivery, and dinner is ready to heat.

Someone going through a hard time. When you do not know what to say, a gift card lets them order comfort on the day they need it most.

Teachers, coworkers, neighbors, and parents. It is a thoughtful thank-you for people who already have a full house.

Employers. You can recognize a team or support an employee with a gift that feels personal. For bulk orders, text or call (412) 998-8404.

This also works beyond December. Birthdays, thank-yous, and "just because" days are all good reasons to send a meal.

How Homemade by Colleen gift cards work

Buying a card takes a few minutes.

  1. Go to the gift cards page and choose a custom amount.
  2. Add a personal note and enter the recipient's email.
  3. They get an email from Gift Up with their gift card and code.

Gift cards expire 12 months after purchase. The remaining balance carries from one order to the next. Recipients can check what is left by entering their code on the gift card page.

We deliver to most of Pittsburgh, with a focus on the South Hills. To confirm an address, go to homemadebycolleen.com, click New Order, and enter the ZIP code.

How to use a gift card at checkout

When the card arrives, here is how to use it.

  1. Go to homemadebycolleen.com.
  2. Click New Order.
  3. Enter your ZIP code.
  4. Add your meals.
  5. At checkout, add your payment option. The gift card code goes in the gift card spot in the payment section.

This part matters. The gift card code does not go in the discount or promo code box. That box will not take a gift card code.

There is no subscription to start or cancel. You can order when you want, which we explain more in Flexible Meal Prep in Pittsburgh: No Subscription Needed. Place your order by Friday at noon to get meals the following week. We deliver on Sunday and Wednesday, by 3 p.m. on your chosen day.

Ideas for choosing an amount

You pick the amount, so the gift can be as small or as generous as you like.

On our meal prep delivery page, main entrees, soups, sides, breakfast, and snacks are listed from $8 to $17. Servings are typically a third larger than many meal prep services. Many customers tell us one meal often feeds two people.

The gift card page also notes that most customers order 5 to 6 meals a week. You can use that as a rough guide when you choose an amount, then look at the current weekly menu before you buy.

If you are buying several cards for a team or a family group, call or text (412) 998-8404 and we can help with a bulk order.
