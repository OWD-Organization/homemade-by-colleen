OUR STORY
At Homemade By Colleen, our culinary journey is rooted in three generations of Italian cooking expertise. From our grandfather’s herb garden wisdom to our grandmother’s masterful baking, and our mother’s culinary artistry, we’ve woven 40 years of daily cooking experience into every meal we prepare. Built on the core values of quality, authenticity, and love, we’ve grown from cherished family recipes to serving thousands of Pittsburgh families with fresh, gourmet meals. Our success comes from treating every meal as if we’re cooking for our own family, using locally-sourced ingredients, and prioritizing food quality over profit.
