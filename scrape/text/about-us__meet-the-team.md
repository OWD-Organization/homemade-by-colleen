The Team
Colleen & Wally - Owners & Culinary Duo
At the heart of Homemade by Colleen are Colleen and Wally, a husband-and-wife team whose passion for food brings warmth to every meal. Drawing from three generations of Italian cooking wisdom, Colleen leads the kitchen with expertise passed down through her family, while Wally ensures smooth operations and timely deliveries. Together, they’ve created a service that feels less like a business and more like an extension of their family kitchen, serving the Pittsburgh community with love in every dish.
Ryan & Jana - Digital Marketing
Ryan and Jana are the dynamic duo behind Homemade by Colleen’s digital presence and business growth. With their combined expertise in digital marketing and customer experience, they’ve helped transform a beloved local kitchen into a thriving meal service. From managing the website and online orders to developing marketing strategies, they ensure that the warmth of Colleen’s kitchen reaches more Pittsburgh families every day, while maintaining the personal touch that makes the service special.
Mike & Benna - Operations Support
Mike and Benna are the backbone of our daily operations, bringing efficiency and care to every aspect of the business. Mike’s dedication to prompt, reliable delivery ensures that each meal reaches its destination at peak freshness, while Benna’s versatility shines through her work in kitchen prep and customer service. Their commitment to excellence and attention to detail help maintain the high standards that our customers have come to expect from Homemade by Colleen.
Rachelle, Dorin, & Kids - Quality Control
Our quality control team, led by Rachelle with support from Dorin and the grandkids, ensures that every dish meets our exacting standards. Rachelle’s seasoned palate and deep understanding of seasonal flavors guide our menu development, while Dorin and the grandkids serve as our trusted taste-testers, providing honest feedback that keeps our offerings fresh and exciting. Their combined expertise helps us maintain the perfect balance of traditional comfort and modern health-conscious cuisine.
