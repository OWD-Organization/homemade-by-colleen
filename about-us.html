Our story

Who We Are

Welcome to “Homemade by Colleen,” where every dish tells a story of tradition, love, and a pinch of Italian heritage. Our journey is as rich and savory as our recipes – a tale woven from the heart of Pittsburgh, PA, where Colleen, a mother not just to her own but to many, infuses generational wisdom into gourmet meals. Colleen’s culinary canvas is painted with the flavors of her childhood, where her families kitchen masterpieces sparked a lifelong passion for cooking. With over 40 years of daily cooking experience, Colleen’s hands have crafted more than just meals; they’ve molded memories, nurtured souls, and fed families.

Meet Homemade By Colleen: Pittsburgh’s Family Meal Prep Experts

Homemade by Colleen blossomed from a simple desire: to secure a future while doing what Colleen loves most – cooking homemade meals in her kitchen. Faced with the challenge of embracing technology and the internet, her son Ryan, a whiz in online marketing, and her supportive husband Wally, turned her dream into a digital reality. Now, Colleen’s prepared meals are just a click away.

Meet the family team at Homemade By Colleen

The Team

Colleen & Wally

Owners & Culinary Duo

At the heart of Homemade by Colleen are Colleen and Wally, a husband-and-wife team whose passion for food brings warmth to every meal. Drawing from three generations of Italian cooking wisdom, Colleen leads the kitchen with expertise passed down through her family, while Wally ensures smooth operations and timely deliveries. Together, they’ve created a service that feels less like a business and more like an extension of their family kitchen, serving the Pittsburgh community with love in every dish.

Ryan & Jana

Digital Marketing

Ryan and Jana are the dynamic duo behind Homemade by Colleen’s digital presence and business growth. With their combined expertise in digital marketing and customer experience, they’ve helped transform a beloved local kitchen into a thriving meal service. From managing the website and online orders to developing marketing strategies, they ensure that the warmth of Colleen’s kitchen reaches more Pittsburgh families every day, while maintaining the personal touch that makes the service special.

Rachelle, Dorin, & Kids

Quality Control

Our quality control team, led by Rachelle with support from Dorin and the grandkids, ensures that every dish meets our exacting standards. Rachelle’s seasoned palate and deep understanding of seasonal flavors guide our menu development, while Dorin and the grandkids serve as our trusted taste-testers, providing honest feedback that keeps our offerings fresh and exciting. Their combined expertise helps us maintain the perfect balance of traditional comfort and modern health-conscious cuisine.

Our Promise

We are more than a homemade meal prep delivery service; Homemade By Colleen is a promise of a healthy home-cooked meals, minus the stress. With over 20,000 meals and 10,000 snacks delivered yearly, we prioritize food quality over profit, choosing local ingredients and crafting a fresh menu inspired by the seasons every week. From winter’s warming stews to summer’s crisp salads, our food evolves with the calendar, always offering something new and nourishing.

The Homemade By Colleen Difference

When you choose Homemade by Colleen, you’re choosing a fundamentally different approach to meal prep delivery. We don’t just promise delicious food – we guarantee a culinary experience rooted in family tradition, premium ingredients, and genuine care. From our weekly changing seasonal menus to our commitment to fresh, never-frozen meals, every aspect of our service is designed to bring the warmth of home cooking to your table.

Your meals are part of the select 30,000+ dishes we lovingly prepare each year, ensuring you receive the quality and attention your family deserves. We maintain this high standard by sourcing ingredients from local suppliers and trusted butchers, not big box companies like Sysco. Every dish is personally overseen in our kitchen, bringing together 40 years of cooking expertise and three generations of cherished family recipes.

Nourishing your family is a precious responsibility, and we stake our reputation on delivering not just satisfying meals, but an experience that gives you time back for what matters most. That’s why we offer flexible ordering with no subscription requirements, accommodate dietary preferences when possible, and ensure every meal is cooked with the same love and care as if we were feeding our own family. Your trust in us means everything, and we prove it with every meal we prepare.
