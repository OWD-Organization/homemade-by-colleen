Questions people always ask

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Just like any good host, I want everyone to feel included. Tell me about allergies, gluten-free needs, vegetarian preferences. Whatever your group requires, we will make it work.

What if my guest count changes?

Life happens. As long as you let me know a few days before your event, we can adjust portions up or down. I have planned enough gatherings to know numbers always shift.

Do you provide serving dishes and utensils?

I provide beautiful, reusable serving containers that you keep (dishwasher and microwave safe). For larger events, I can arrange full service including serving staff if needed.

What if something goes wrong with my order?

In 8+ years of cooking for Pittsburgh families and events, I can count problems on one hand. If something is not right, I will make it right immediately. Your event matters to me.

How far in advance should I book?

One week minimum gives me time to source the best ingredients and prepare everything properly. For graduations, holidays, corporate events, and peak seasons, earlier is always better.

Are your portions really satisfying?

I cook like I am feeding people I care about. Nobody leaves hungry. My portions are generous because I know how disappointing it is when there is not enough food at a celebration.
