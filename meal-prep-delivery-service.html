Frequently Asked Questions About Our Local Meal Prep Service

How do you keep everything so fresh? Other meal services I've tried seemed mass-produced...

Just like you would at home, we shop multiple times a week from local markets and suppliers. No big box suppliers here! Every meal is prepared fresh weekly in small batches – never frozen, never stockpiled. It's the same care you'd take cooking for your own family.

Can you work around dietary restrictions and allergies?

Absolutely! Every family has different needs, and we're happy to accommodate them. Whether you need fewer carbs, extra veggies, or no cheese, just text me personally at 412-515-6445. We'll work together to make sure everyone at your table can enjoy their meal.

Why should I choose this over just going to the grocery store?

Think of us as your family's personal chef and meal planner combined:

  • We handle everything from planning to cleanup
  • No more wilted produce or forgotten ingredients
  • You get restaurant-quality meals without the restaurant prices
  • Every ingredient is thoughtfully sourced and freshly prepared
  • You save hours of shopping, cooking, and cleaning time
Is there a commitment? What if I need to skip a week?

No contracts, no obligations – just good food when you want it! Simply order when you need us and skip when you don't. Life gets busy, and we get that. We'll be here with fresh meals whenever you're ready to order again.

What makes Homemade by Colleen different from other meal prep services?

The difference is in the details:

  • Three generations of family recipes and cooking wisdom
  • Local, seasonal ingredients (no big-box suppliers)
  • Weekly changing menu that keeps meals exciting
  • Portions generous enough to often feed two
  • Personal touch you can only get from a family-run kitchen
I've had bland meal prep before – how's the taste?

After 40 years of cooking for family and friends, I've learned one thing: food made with love just tastes different. Every dish is crafted with the same care I use cooking for my own family. Plus, our rotating menu keeps things exciting with new dishes every week. If anything ever isn't perfect, just let me know – your satisfaction matters to us!

Do I need to be home for delivery?

Not at all! Just leave a cooler outside, and our friendly delivery team will carefully place your meals inside and text you once they're delivered. It's that simple!

Can I try it out first?

Start with just two meals to get a taste of what we offer. While we don't provide free samples (we're a small, family-run business), our minimum order is low enough to let you try us out without a big commitment. Check out our customer reviews to see why families keep coming back!

Those portions look generous – but are they really enough?
